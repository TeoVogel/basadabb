# BasadaBB

A community-curated directory of where to find vegan food in Bahía Blanca, Argentina. Domain terms are kept in Spanish, the language of the site and of its curators.

## Language

**Local**:
A business with a physical location in the city where you can go to eat or buy vegan food, whether it is fully vegan or only has some vegan options. Each branch of a chain is its own Local.
_Avoid_: Place, restaurant, shop, lugar

**Emprendimiento**:
A small vegan food business with no physical location to visit, usually producing to order rather than holding stock, and reached through social media or delivery.
_Avoid_: Shop, tienda, delivery

**Tipo**:
The kind of business a Local is (for example Café, Heladería, Rotisería), chosen from a fixed list the Curadores maintain.
_Avoid_: Category, rubro

**Opciones veganas**:
The free-text description of what a Local or Emprendimiento offers that is vegan.
_Avoid_: Menu, carta

**Verificación**:
The date a Curador last confirmed that a Local's Opciones veganas are still accurate.
_Avoid_: Last updated, fecha de actualización

**Curador**:
A member of the small group allowed to add and edit Locales and Emprendimientos.
_Avoid_: Admin, editor, moderator
